How information flows across the rest home

In New Zealand residential aged care, clinical safety depends on a clear chain of responsibility from care staff to the Registered Nurse, Facility Manager, GP, and Whānau.

  1. 1. Carer Shift & ADL Recording (/carer, /handover) Health Care Assistants record daily care tasks (meals, fluids, mobility, hygiene), spot vital signs, and shift concerns, then send a structured shift handover to the Registered Nurse.
  2. 2. Registered Nurse Clinical Triage & Verification (/nurse, /ocr, /guidance) The Nurse Dashboard combines NZEWS vital-sign rules with local AI note analysis to rank residents Red, Amber, or Green. Nurses verify printed hospital/lab reports via OCR, approve RAG care guidance, and generate 1-page ISBAR Doctor Summary PDFs.
  3. 3. Facility Manager Governance & Medicine Audit (/manager) Facility Managers oversee staff accounts, review the clinical audit log, and track scheduled medicine rounds and clinical sign-offs in line with NZ Health and Disability Services Standards (NZS 8134).
  4. 4. Whānau / Family Daily Updates (/family) Families receive plain-language daily wellbeing updates that a Registered Nurse has reviewed and approved first, with direct messaging to the care team.

Designed for every role in New Zealand aged care

Health Care Assistants (Carers)

Record daily care & shift handovers in a few taps

Select a resident to log Activities of Daily Living (ADLs), spot vital signs, or flag a concern for the Registered Nurse on duty.

  • Clear shift task buttons built for mobile and tablet use on the floor
  • Displays only care guidance that a Registered Nurse has checked and approved
  • Vital signs outside normal range automatically escalate to the Nurse Dashboard

Registered Nurses (RNs)

Clinical triage, OCR report reader, & GP handover

Each resident receives an NZEWS + AI care priority with full source traceability. Nurses accept or override priorities with a clinical reason recorded in the audit log.

  • OCR Health Report Reader: Extract vital signs from printed discharge or lab reports, verify every value against the photo, and save to the resident's record
  • RAG Care Guidance: Review source-quoted care guidance lines before carers see them
  • Doctor Summary PDF & Camera Check: Export 1-page GP handover summaries and review privacy-first room/discomfort alerts

Whānau & Families

Reassuring daily updates checked by a Registered Nurse

A warm, plain-language summary of your family member's day — meals, comfort, and activities — reviewed by a nurse before publication, plus direct care-team messaging.

  • Plain language with no raw clinical scores or unreviewed AI output
  • Direct message channel to the nursing team with urgent-concern flagging

Clinical governance & privacy safeguards

  1. Nurse-in-the-loop authorityAI suggests priorities and drafts guidance; a Registered Nurse must verify, edit, or approve every clinical decision before it takes effect.
  2. Source-grounded care guidanceEvery guidance line quotes an approved clinical reference note. Any line mentioning prescription medicines or medical diagnoses is automatically blocked.
  3. On-device AI & OCR processingLanguage models (Ollama) and optical character recognition (RapidOCR) run locally on the facility server so resident notes stay on-premise.
  4. Zero raw photo retentionUploaded report photos and camera frames are processed in memory and immediately discarded after extraction.
  5. Full clinical audit trailEvery priority decision, OCR vital-sign save, handover sign-off, and guidance approval is logged with staff name and timestamp.
  6. NZ standards alignmentAligned with NZEWS vital-sign thresholds, ISBAR clinical communication, and NZ Health Information Privacy Code principles.
