Registered nurses

Know who needs you first

Each resident gets a suggested Red, Amber, or Green priority with the reasons and the carer's notes. Accept it or change it with a reason in the audit log.

  • Check numbers read from printed health reports before saving
  • Approve or edit AI care guidance before carers see it
  • Review shift handovers, camera alerts, and print a 1-page doctor summary

Families

A short, simple update about your family member, checked by a nurse first, and a way to message the care team.

  • Plain language with no confusing medical scores
  • Direct message box to reach the nursing team

Safety rules

  1. The nurse decidesThe AI suggests a priority and drafts guidance. A registered nurse accepts, changes, or rejects it.
  2. Every line has a sourceGuidance lines must quote an approved reference note. Lines that mention medicines or diagnoses are removed.
  3. AI runs locallyThe language model and report reader run on this computer. Resident notes are not sent to a cloud AI service.
  4. Photos are not savedReport photos and camera frames are read in memory and then discarded right away.
  5. Audit log for every actionEvery priority check, saved report reading, handover, and guidance approval is recorded with the staff member's name and time.
  6. Built for New Zealand careUses New Zealand Early Warning Score (NZEWS) vital-sign rules and clear shift handovers.
