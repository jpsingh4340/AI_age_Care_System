AI Age-Care Intelligence System · student prototype

Carers record the shift. The nurse sees who needs them first.

AgeCare turns carer notes and vital signs into a Red, Amber or Green care priority. A local AI suggests; the nurse makes the final decision.

Who it is for

Carers

Record care in a few taps

Pick a resident, then save a task, a set of vital signs, or a concern for the nurse.

  • Large buttons that work on a phone
  • Only guidance the nurse has approved
  • Readings outside the usual range go to the nurse

Registered nurses

Know who needs you first

Each resident gets a suggested priority with the reasons and the carer's own words. Accept it or change it with a reason; both go in the audit log.

  • Approve or edit AI guidance before carers see it
  • Check numbers read from printed health reports
  • Decide on face and room check alerts

Families

A daily update you can trust

A short, plain update about your family member, checked by a nurse first, and a way to message the care team.

  • No clinical scores or AI output
  • Opening hours and who to contact

Safety rules

  1. The nurse decidesThe AI suggests a priority and drafts guidance. A registered nurse accepts, changes or rejects it.
  2. Every line has a sourceGuidance lines must quote an approved reference note. Lines that mention medicines or diagnoses are removed.
  3. AI runs locallyThe language model runs with Ollama on this computer. Notes are not sent to a cloud AI service.
  4. Photos are not savedReport photos, face photos and video frames are read in memory and then discarded.
  5. Demo data onlyAll residents, staff and readings are fake.
  6. Not a medical deviceThis is a student prototype for learning, not for real care.
