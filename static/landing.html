Who It Is For

For Carers

Quick, touch-friendly shift sheets to log daily tasks, spot vitals, and shift concerns in seconds. Clear access to nurse-approved guidance with zero clinical clutter.

For Registered Nurses

Clear triage answering "Who needs me first?", source-backed guidance drafts, health report optical character reading, and final review authority over all care decisions.

For Whānau & Families

A calm, reassuring portal providing warm daily updates approved by the nurse, with a direct way to message the care team. No frightening jargon or raw clinical scores.

Safety and Clinical Control Rules

This prototype operates under strict healthcare guardrails:

  • Nurse in the loop: The AI never diagnoses or prescribes medicine. The Registered Nurse makes the final decision on all care priorities and guidance.
  • Source-grounded: Every guidance line must cite an approved clinical reference note with exact quote matching.
  • Local and private: The small language model (Ollama) runs entirely on facility hardware. Resident information is never sent to cloud AI providers.
  • Zero photo retention: Images and camera frames are analysed in volatile memory and immediately discarded. Photos are not saved.
  • Fictional demonstration data: All residents, health conditions, and staff names are synthetic demonstration records.
