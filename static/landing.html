The responsible AI journey

From trusted source
to reviewed guidance.

Our approach is designed so that AI proposes, safety rules filter, and qualified humans make care decisions.

01

Gather care information

Use resident care plans and approved reference notes.

02

Draft source-linked notes

03

Apply safety checks

Automatically remove unsupported lines and disallowed medical advice.

04

Nurse reviews
