AgeCare

Privacy

How this student prototype handles information.

Student prototype

AgeCare is a student project for Studio 5. It is not used in real care and is not a medical device.

Fake data only

All residents, staff, readings, notes and contacts are made up. Do not enter real health information.

AI runs locally

The language model runs with Ollama on the computer running AgeCare. Notes and questions are not sent to a cloud AI service.

Photos are not stored

Report photos, face photos and video frames are read in memory and then discarded. Only the result, any alert and the nurse's decision are saved.

Who sees what

Each person sees only the pages for their role. Families see only nurse-approved updates for their own family member. Sign-offs and account changes are kept in an audit log.
