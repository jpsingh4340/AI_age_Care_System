AgeCare

Terms of use

Please read these before you try the prototype.

Clinical responsibility

AgeCare supports carers, nurses, managers, and families. It does not replace a registered nurse or doctor for diagnosis or treatment.

The nurse makes the final decision

Care priorities, guidance and report readings from the system are suggestions. A registered nurse checks and decides before anything reaches carers or families.

Fake data only

Use only the demo residents. Do not upload photos or videos of real residents. Test media must show project volunteers who agreed.

AI runs locally, photos are not stored

The AI runs with Ollama on this computer. Photos and video frames are discarded after they are read.

Audit log

Sign-offs, priority changes and account changes are recorded with the name of the person who made them.
