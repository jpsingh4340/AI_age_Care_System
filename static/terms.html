Prototype disclaimer and conditions of use

Not a Medical Device: This software is a student project demonstration. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

1. Purpose of System

The AI Age-Care Intelligence System is developed solely for educational and research evaluation by student researchers. It provides experimental workflows for shift handover organisation, optical character reading of printed charts, and local language model summarisation.

2. Registered Nurse Authority

All automated outputs, care priority suggestions, and extracted readings are advisory only. A Registered Nurse maintains complete decision-making authority and must verify every record before it is saved or communicated to carers or families.

3. Demonstration Use Only

Users must not input real identifiable health records or upload photographs of real patients. Demonstration files must feature synthetic data or consenting project volunteers.

4. Auditability

Actions performed by authenticated staff members, including priority changes, guidance approvals, and user administration, are recorded in a permanent audit log for accountability.
