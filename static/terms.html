Terms of use

How staff and families use the AgeCare platform.

Clinical responsibility

AgeCare supports carers, nurses, managers, and families. It does not replace a registered nurse or doctor for diagnosis or treatment.

The nurse makes the final decision

Care priorities, guidance, and report readings from the system are suggestions. A registered nurse checks and decides before anything reaches carers or families.

AI runs locally, photos are not stored

The AI and OCR reader run locally on this computer. Photos and video frames are discarded immediately after they are read.

Audit log

Sign-offs, priority changes, saved report values, and account changes are recorded with the name of the person who made them.
